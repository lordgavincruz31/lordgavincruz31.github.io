ELECTROMECHANICAL

> SYMPTOM: Unit experiences random shut-offs mid-cycle or refuses to engage the spin basin despite indicator lights functioning correctly.

> DIAGNOSTICS: Traced input power loops using a digital multimeter to confirm solid wall-voltage feed. Inspected structural mechanical elements—the main drive belt tension was optimal, but checking the door/lid interlock switch switch revealed unstable continuity readings when locked.

> RESOLUTION: Unplugged mains power supply lines and opened the control cowl array. Extracted the degraded safety switch unit and cleaned internal physical oxidation layers from the terminal blades.
