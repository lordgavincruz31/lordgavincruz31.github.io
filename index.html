05 // Troubleshooting Lab Logs

> SYMPTOM: Laptop screen gradually turned white with green lines during a gaming session until it covered the entire display.

> DIAGNOSTICS: Interviewed the customer, who noted a lower horizontal line had been present for a long time. Inspected the internals and found heavy dust buildup, leading to overheating that permanently burned out the LCD layer.

> RESOLUTION: Removed the damaged LCD panel, verified its part number, and ordered a direct replacement online. Installed the new screen while deep-cleaning the internal components and replacing the dried factory thermal paste with high-quality **Arctic MX6** compound.

> SYMPTOM: Laptop takes an excessive amount of time to boot up and responds slowly during initial loading phases.

> DIAGNOSTICS: Checked system specifications and confirmed the device was running on a slow mechanical HDD. Storage was completely full, and the current operating system configuration was cluttered.

> RESOLUTION: Recommended an SSD upgrade, but adjusted to a budget alternative at the client's request. Backed up user files, performed a fresh installation of Windows 10, configured essential drivers, cleaned out the dust, and applied fresh thermal paste to improve responsiveness.

> SYMPTOM: Fan completely refuses to spin when turned on, showing no signs of blade movement.

> DIAGNOSTICS: Tested the main power lines for continuity using a digital multimeter and confirmed electricity was flowing. Detected faint motor housing vibrations when plugged in, diagnosing a physically locked shaft and dried-up bushings.

> RESOLUTION: Disassembled the motor assembly cage. Removed the seized front and rear sleeve bushings along with the worn center shaft. Replaced them with fresh matching components, applied premium machine oil to the wells, and verified free rotation.

> SYMPTOM: Unit does not power on at all; status indicator lights remain entirely dark upon connection.

> DIAGNOSTICS: Checked the main power plug with a multimeter probe and found no circuit continuity. Opened the bottom panel shell and isolated a blown thermal safety fuse.

> RESOLUTION: Located heavily rusted wires connected to the main heating component that were causing loose connections and electrical resistance. Cut away the corroded wiring, re-terminated the contacts cleanly, and replaced the blown thermal fuse to restore power safely.

> SYMPTOM: The spin-dryer tub functions normally, but the main washing tub fails to rotate or stay engaged.

> DIAGNOSTICS: Noted that the physical wash timer dial felt loose and would spring back immediately. Verified wiring continuity from the plug to the internal back panel with a multimeter, confirming that the electrical path was intact but the timer dial was broken mechanically.

> RESOLUTION: Opened the rear control housing plate and disengaged the failed mechanical timer piece. Wired in a brand new matching wash timer replacement unit and tested operation; the cycle loops now hold contacts perfectly.

> SYMPTOM: Air conditioning unit creates a loud, shaking noise and intense rattling sounds during normal running operations.

> DIAGNOSTICS: Suspected a compressor issue and applied standard service check procedures. Removed the cover sleeve, measured the temperature differences (Delta-T validation), and checked operating amperage loads; electrical and mechanical baselines were normal.

> RESOLUTION: Discovered that the original shipping/transportation foams inside the internal fan tracks were never removed, causing the fan blades to strike them. Cleared out the blockages and reassembled the sleeve casing; marked the unit as **Class B / Perfect Condition** with sound levels fully quieted.
